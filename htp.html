How to Play

Welcome to P i x e l P r o g r e s s i o n !

- Inspired by games like RobTopGames' Geometry Dash and Aniwey's Candy Box, P i x e l P r o g r e s s i o n is a 2D platformer focused on vertical movement.
- Use left arrow and right arrow to move, and space to jump, but be careful of enemies!
- There are also power up scattered around some levels that all have their own special effect. Can you figure out what they do?
